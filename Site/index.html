The volcano erupts. Your cart catches the coins.

An arcade game for iPhone and iPad. The crater fires volleys of glowing coins and lava bombs down its slope. Roll your mine cart along two rails, catch the coins, dodge the bombs, smash the obsidian they leave behind and bank your hot haul before the cart overheats.

About

A vault under the crater

A living volcano

The crater is the star: it glows before every volley, fills the sky with ash and throws more, faster, the longer you last.

A cave to explore

The vault is your menu: brew upgrades on the assay shelf, hire keepers from the lantern row, and read every shift in the core sample.

Earned, never bought

Gold comes only from play. No ads, no purchases, no accounts — Crater Hoard works completely offline.

How it works

Drag, swipe, tap

Drag

Roll the cart under the shadows of falling coins and away from the red shadows of lava bombs.

Swipe

Jump between the lower and upper rail. Tap to dash through obsidian boulders.

Bank

Roll into the mine entrance at the end of the rail to cool the cart and bank its coins. Spend gold on upgrades and new keepers.

Signature mechanics

Five rules of the slope

Telegraphed volleys

The crater glows gold for coins, red for bombs and both for a mix. Growing shadows mark where everything lands.

Hot cargo

Caught coins heat the cart. Carry more for a bigger multiplier, but bank them at the mine before a bomb or the heat takes them.

Obsidian crust

Bombs you dodge harden into boulders that block the rail. Dash through them for shards of gold.

Two rails

The upper rail is richer and hides volcanic cores, but it's hotter and more dangerous. Jumping between rails leaves you exposed.

Ash cloud

Big volleys darken the sky and fade the shadows — but the thick cloud sparks lightning that pays to catch.

Support

Need a hand at the claw?

Write to support@craterhoard.site — we usually answer within two working days.

How do I control the cart?

Drag to roll along the rail, swipe up or down to jump to the other rail, and tap to dash. The on-screen UP/DOWN and DASH levers do the same.

Why did my coins disappear?

A lava bomb knocks the cargo out of the cart, and an overheated cart melts it. Bank often at the mine entrance at the end of your rail.

What ends a shift?

The cart has wooden planks. Each bomb hit or overheat cracks one. When the last plank breaks, the shift ends and the gold you banked is yours.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Gold, upgrades, keepers and your last 24 shifts survive restarts. You can reset everything in the Valve Room.

Does the game cost anything or show ads?

No. There are no ads and no purchases. Gold can only be earned by playing.

Can I play without sound?

Yes. Sound and haptics can each be switched off in the Valve Room, and the game respects the silent switch.
