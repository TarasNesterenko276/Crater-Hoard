About

A vault under the crater

Real physics

Every coin has its own size and weight. Stack carelessly and the hoard slides, tips and tumbles into the lava.

A cave to explore

The vault is your menu: brew upgrades on the assay shelf, hire keepers from the lantern row, and read every shift in the core sample.

Earned, never bought

Gold comes only from play. No ads, no purchases, no accounts — Crater Hoard works completely offline.

How it works

One finger, three moves

Drag

Slide anywhere to move the claw over the beam. The next coin is always shown in the corner.

Release

Lift your finger to drop. Every coin that lands adds gold — more while the beam stays level.

Bank & upgrade

When the lava reaches the beam the shift ends. Spend the gold on upgrades and new keepers, then ride up again.

Signature mechanics

Four rules of the crater

Weld heat

Fresh coins are molten and cool in the claw. Drop one inside the bright band and it welds to the coin below — but wait too long and the claw lets go.

Minting

Three coins of one metal stacked straight up fuse into a wide, flat ingot. Bonus gold, a sturdier base, and the lava cools a notch.

Fulcrum beam

The beam balances on a single rock. Weight off-centre tips it. Land coins while the dial is in the green to grow the steady multiplier.

Lava creep

Every spilled coin sinks with its gold and raises the lava a notch. When the lava touches the beam, the shift is over.

Support

Write to support@__DOMAIN__ — we usually answer within two working days.

How do I weld coins?

Watch the heat bar above the claw. Drop while the bar is inside the bright band and the coin welds to the coin it lands on. Coins never weld to the bare beam.

Why did three coins turn into a bar?

That's minting: three coins of the same metal stacked directly on top of each other fuse into an ingot. Obsidian is volcanic glass and never mints.

What ends a shift?

Each coin that falls into the lava raises it one notch. When the lava reaches the beam, the shift ends and your gold is banked.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Gold, upgrades, keepers and your last 24 shifts survive restarts. You can reset everything in the Valve Room.

Does the game cost anything or show ads?

No. There are no ads and no purchases. Gold can only be earned by playing.

Can I play without sound?

Yes. Sound and haptics can each be switched off in the Valve Room, and the game respects the silent switch.
